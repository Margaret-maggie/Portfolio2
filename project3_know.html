Support Operations & Escalation Prevention

Building proactive communication protocols and ticket management systems at KNOW App that eliminated escalations while handling high-volume enterprise support.

Context

At KNOW App, enterprise clients rely on the platform for critical business operations. When configuration issues, workflow errors, or product defects arise, fast and effective support is essential. I was responsible for managing the support queue alongside onboarding duties — typically handling 50 to 80 tickets per week across a growing base of enterprise accounts.

The risk was clear: without a structured approach, high ticket volumes would lead to delayed responses, frustrated clients, and escalations that could damage relationships and increase churn. My goal was to keep every client satisfied while maintaining operational efficiency.

Problems Identified

Reactive Support Culture

Tickets were handled as they arrived with no prioritization framework. Critical issues from enterprise clients sat in the same queue as minor queries, causing delays.

No Early Warning System

There was no structured way to spot patterns — recurring issues, at-risk accounts, or clients submitting multiple tickets — until they became serious problems.

Inconsistent Communication

Clients were often left waiting for updates. Different team members communicated differently, creating confusion and eroding trust.

Knowledge Gaps

Without documented resolution paths, the same issues were solved from scratch each time, wasting time and producing inconsistent outcomes.

Approach

I designed a support operations framework focused on prevention, consistency, and speed. The initiative ran in parallel with onboarding work and became the backbone of our client operations.

01

Ticket Analysis & Categorization

  • Audited 8 weeks of ticket data to identify top issue categories and frequency patterns
  • Created a tiered priority system: P1 (enterprise blocker), P2 (workflow issue), P3 (config query), P4 (general question)
  • Mapped average resolution times by category to set realistic internal targets
  • Identified 6 recurring issue types responsible for 60% of total ticket volume
02

Proactive Communication Protocols

  • Built response time SLAs by tier: P1 within 2 hours, P2 within 4 hours, P3 within 8 hours, P4 within 24 hours
  • Created status update templates for clients so they knew what to expect at every stage
  • Introduced weekly check-in emails for enterprise accounts with open tickets
  • Set up internal alerts when a client submitted 3+ tickets in 7 days, triggering a proactive outreach
03

Resolution Playbooks & Self-Service

  • Documented step-by-step resolution guides for the top 6 recurring issues
  • Created client-facing quick-reference guides for common configuration tasks
  • Built an internal knowledge base in Confluence for the support team
  • Trained the team on playbook usage to ensure consistent, fast resolutions
04

Tracking & Continuous Improvement

  • Built a tracking sheet in Excel to monitor ticket volumes, resolution times, and client satisfaction signals
  • Reviewed metrics weekly to spot trends and adjust priorities
  • Shared insights with product and engineering to fix root causes upstream
  • Refined SOPs based on team feedback and client outcomes
Execution Details
  • Ticket Triage: Every incoming ticket was categorized within 30 minutes using the new priority framework. Enterprise blockers were immediately flagged and assigned.
  • Client Communication: I personally drafted and sent all P1 and P2 status updates to enterprise clients, ensuring they never had to ask for information.
  • Cross-functional Coordination: When tickets revealed product bugs or feature gaps, I logged detailed Jira tickets with reproduction steps and client impact, then followed through to closure.
  • Team Training: Conducted weekly 30-minute sessions with the support team on new playbooks, sharing wins and addressing edge cases.
  • Feedback Loop: After resolving complex issues, I sent brief satisfaction check-ins to enterprise clients, using their input to refine our approach.

Key Learnings
  • Speed without communication feels like silence. Clients care as much about knowing what is happening as they do about the fix itself. A timely update prevents 80% of frustration.
  • Patterns reveal root causes. Tracking ticket categories showed us that 60% of issues came from just 6 root causes. Fixing those upstream reduced volume significantly.
  • Documentation is a force multiplier. Playbooks did not just speed up resolutions — they made the entire team consistent. A new team member could handle enterprise tickets on day three, not week three.
  • Proactive outreach builds trust. When we reached out to clients before they had to ask, the relationship shifted from transactional to partnership. Zero escalations was a byproduct of that trust.
"The best escalation is the one that never happens. That requires seeing around corners — and that only works when you have systems that surface signals early."
